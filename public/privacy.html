Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

What we collect

When you create an account we store your email address and a display username. Everything you log in the app - hands, positions, actions, results, win and loss amounts, buy-ins, cash-outs, and session notes - is saved to your account so it can appear in your history and stats.

Discord

If you choose to connect Discord, we store your Discord user ID and username so "Share" posts can mention you, and we post a one-line summary of a completed session to our community channel when you ask us to. We never post hand-by-hand details.

How we use it

Your data is used only to run the app for you: coach replies, session tracking, stats, leaderboards among friends you add, and post-session recaps. We never sell or share personal data with third parties for advertising.

Where it's stored

Data is hosted on Supabase (PostgreSQL) and processed by our API on Vercel. AI analysis is sent to a hosted large language model (Groq) to generate coaching advice; it is not used to train their models.

Local storage

Your browser stores your login session and a small flag remembering whether you've seen the intro. You can clear these by clearing your browser data for the site.

Your choices

You can delete individual hands, delete whole sessions, and remove your Discord connection at any time. To delete your account and all its data, email the account email used to log in - requests are processed within 30 days.
